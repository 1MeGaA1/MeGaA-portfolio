ABOUT US

OUR MISSION

"To provide high-quality audio production and creative support for emerging artists to help them reach their full potential."

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Do I need to book an engineer, or can I bring my own?

All our sessions include one of our expert in-house engineers to ensure you get the best sound from our hardware. If you wish to bring an outside engineer, please let us know in advance so we can facilitate the technical handover.

Do you offer "Mix and Master only" services?

Yes! If you've recorded elsewhere, you can send us your stems. We provide professional mixing and mastering using industry-standard tools (Ozone, FabFilter, Waves) to ensure your track is radio-ready and hits the top charts.

How does your distribution service work?

We offer two simple paths to get your music on Spotify, Apple Music, and Anghami:

  • Single Release: 200 L.E per track.
  • Annual Unlimited: 1,500 L.E per year for unlimited releases for a single artist.

Both packages include basic data analytics to help you track your performance.

What should I bring to my recording session?

Please bring your backing tracks/beats on a USB drive or have them accessible via a cloud link (Google Drive / WeTransfer). If you have a specific "vocal reference" or a song that has the "vibe" you want, bring that too — it helps our engineers match your vision faster!

How long do you keep my project files?

We maintain a backup of your session files for 90 days after completion. We highly recommend that all artists bring their own hard drive to take their stems and session data home immediately after the final bounce.
