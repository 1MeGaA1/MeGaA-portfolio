The Hotel

One hundred years
of stillness.

Auriga has stood on this coast for a century — a former private estate of the Marquez family, reimagined in 1982 as a hotel of just thirty-two rooms.

The architecture remains: limestone walls, cedar shutters, and gardens designed to frame the sea rather than compete with it. The cooking is built around what the boats bring in each morning. The service, on the rare occasion you require it, is unhurried.

We are not a resort. We are a home that opens its doors a few months each year.

Isabel Marquez Owner & Host · 3rd generation
